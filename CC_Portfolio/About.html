About Me

Hi! I'm Alice, a digital artist and designer currently pursuing my MFA in Design and Technology at Parsons School of Design. I graduated from Parsons in 2024 with a BFA, and my creative practice focuses on digital illustration, character design, and game art.

I enjoy creating imaginative worlds and characters that explore themes of childhood, emotions, and human relationships. I'm especially interested in combining visual storytelling with interactive experiences, often using playful and whimsical aesthetics to communicate deeper ideas.

While my background is primarily in visual art, I've recently been exploring creative coding and game development through tools like p5.js and Unity. Through these experiences, I hope to expand my creative possibilities and discover new ways to bring my illustrations and stories to life.
