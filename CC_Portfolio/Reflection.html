Reflection: My Journey with Creative Coding

Before taking CCLab, my creative practice was primarily focused on digital illustration and visual design. Coding was something I had very little experience with, and I often found it difficult to understand how different parts of a program worked together.

Throughout this semester, I learned how to use variables, conditional statements, loops, arrays, functions, and classes to create interactive visual experiences. One of my biggest challenges was understanding the structure and order of code, especially when working with nested loops and multiple conditions. However, through experimentation, debugging, and revisiting my mistakes, I gradually became more comfortable with programming.

My favorite part of this class was discovering how coding could bring my illustrations and characters to life. Instead of creating only static images, I could now experiment with animation, interaction, and storytelling.

Although coding is still challenging for me, this class helped me see it as another creative tool rather than simply a technical skill. Moving forward, I hope to continue exploring how creative coding can support my interests in illustration and game design.
